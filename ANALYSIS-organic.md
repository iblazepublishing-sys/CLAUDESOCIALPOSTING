## What it says
1. Kidney/dialysis is the audience. Every top post is kidney. Iron, nerves, lymph do a fraction.
2. "What nobody tells you" hooks win: a hidden truth or a number people misunderstand.
3. Lab-literacy posts (eGFR, ACR) earn comments and saves without big claims.
4. Engagement is ~0.1% of followers. Most of the 234k don't see posts; reach is coming from non-followers via Reels.
5. 6 Reels in 5 hours on one account splits reach. Later posts get less.

## Plan
- Per account: 1–2 Reels/day, plus 3–5 Stories/day (Stories are where volume goes).
- Mix per week (7–10 Reels): 40% "what they don't tell you" kidney truths, 25% lab literacy (one number explained),
  20% client stories with lab summaries, 15% daily life with CKD (fluid limits, food, dialysis days). Offers in Stories, not Reels.
- Other products (iron, nerves, lymph, gut): test on @cwbotanics / @cwbotanicshealth, keep @cosmiccellfoods kidney-first.
- One comment keyword for all posts (REVERSAL) so the autoresponder stays simple.
- Same video on several accounts the same day: Instagram ranks reposted/duplicate videos lower. Space them a day+ apart or re-cut the hook.
